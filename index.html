Kannur Fitness Wisdom
🌅 Morning ritual: Warm jeera water + soaked almonds before any food — kickstarts metabolism, zero sugar.
🍚 Rice upgrade: Switch to Matta (red Kerala) rice — lower GI, more fibre, keeps you full 40% longer than white rice.
🐟 Fish first: Sardine, Tuna, Mackerel (Ayala) — the cheapest, leanest protein in Kannur. Curry over deep fry.
☕ Chai math: 4 cups Kerala chai (full milk+sugar) = 440 kcal. Switch to Sulaimani or black tea and save those calories for real food.
💪 Gym + diet: Without diet, even 1 hour gym only burns ~300 kcal. What you eat matters 80%. Track every meal.
🥗 Protein target: For muscle building, aim 1.6–2g protein per kg bodyweight. A 70kg person needs 112–140g/day.
⚠️ Bakery trap: Egg puff 295 · Chicken puff 325 · Parotta 310 · Sharjah shake 345 kcal. Love them, but count them.
⚠️ Weekend reset: One cheat meal is fine. One cheat day = 1000–2000 extra kcal = almost 1 week of progress erased.
